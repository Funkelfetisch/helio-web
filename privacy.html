Your enquiry

When you submit the enquiry form or send us an email, we receive the contact details and project information you provide. We use them to answer your request, prepare a possible quote, or support existing equipment. We process enquiries to take steps at your request before a contract, to fulfil an existing contract, or for our legitimate interest in answering general correspondence.

Form submissions are stored in access-controlled Cloudflare D1 storage and receive a reference confirming that the server accepted the enquiry. During form processing, ordinary enquiry rows older than 180 days are removed unless the information has been transferred into records required for an ongoing business relationship, contract, legal obligation or dispute. Email correspondence is retained while needed for the same purposes. Access is limited to the business owner and authorized service administration. Please avoid sending payment-card details or unnecessary personal information.

Spam protection and hosting

The form uses a hidden field and short-term rate limits. For rate limiting, the connecting IP address is converted into a salted one-way fingerprint; the raw address is not stored in the enquiry database. During form processing, rate-limit records older than two days are removed. The form does not use a CAPTCHA, advertising tracker or marketing cookie.

Cloudflare hosts this website and processes the form. Hosting and network providers may process technical connection information, including IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect it. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy for information about its processing and international operations. External links and your email service operate under their own privacy policies.

This website

The site does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not embed third-party fonts, videos or tracking scripts. A successful form submission is stored as an enquiry; ordinary page views and clicks are not added to the enquiry database.
